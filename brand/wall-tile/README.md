# Wall tile chips

Wall-only tile. Anything floor-rated lives in `brand/flooring/` instead, because a floor tile can
go on a wall and a wall tile cannot go on a floor - so the floor catalog is also the wall catalog,
and this folder only holds what floor-rated stock cannot cover.

## Supergres — Bricksy (8 colors)

6x24cm (2.5"x9.5") glossy brick format: Talc, Ash, Dove, Indigo, Ocean, Agata, Emerald, Coral.

Each chip is a square cut from the middle of the laid-up panel on the catalog's range page, not a
single brick - the panel is what the owner is actually choosing, grout joints and all. The 224
individual brick images on that page are clustered to the caption beneath them, and the crop
refuses to run on a cluster of fewer than twenty.

## Supergres — INX, white-body wall tile (7 colors)

50x120cm (20"x48"), 8.5mm: Loto, Dune, Jasmine, Agave, Plume, Aqua, Sugar.

These are different colors from INX's floor porcelain (Cement, Grey, White, Pearl, Ivory, Clay), so
the wall range adds seven colors that cannot be had floor-rated. That is why it is worth loading.

The Stripe, Stripe Cold Mix, Stripe Warm Mix and Camp. Voile items on the same page are decors, not
base colors, and are matched out by name.

## Colovers+ is deliberately not here

Its white-body wall tile repeats colors that already exist in the floor-rated porcelain range. A
floor tile goes on a wall, so loading them would put two rows with the same color name in front of
an owner, one of them strictly less useful. Colovers belongs in the flooring catalog only.
