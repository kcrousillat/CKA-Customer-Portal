# Brochure chips

Cropped from the manufacturer brochures at 2x and named to match their rows in the **Palettes**
table — `pgt-frame-bronze.png` is PGT / Frame color / Bronze.

Drag each into the **Photo** field on its Palettes row. Attach once here and every future job
inherits the image when its palette is copied in.

These are brochure crops, good enough to decide from on screen. If a rep supplies a better
color-card image, replace the attachment on the Palettes row — nothing else has to change.

No chips exist for **grid styles** or **door hardware styles**: the brochure shows those as line
drawings and small product photos rather than swatches.
