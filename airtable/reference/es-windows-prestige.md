Named hardware in the line: **Aria** handle (swing doors), **Crest** and **Invisio** (sliding glass

## Not in this brochure

**Glass tints.** The Prestige brochure is system- and section-focused; it carries no tint palette
the way PGT's does. Get the glass options from the ES rep before the glass decision is put to an
owner — do not assume PGT's list applies.

## Where the two brands differ, and why it matters

- ES offers **woodgrain**, PGT does not. Seven of them.
- **Bronze is not the same color.** ES bronze samples near-black (`#1A190D`); PGT bronze is a
  warm dark brown (`#454035`). An owner who approved "bronze" against the wrong palette has
  approved the wrong window — which is exactly why the color decision waits on the manufacturer.
- PGT has **Clear Anodized**; ES has **Silver** and **Champagne Bronze**. Neither list is a subset
  of the other.
