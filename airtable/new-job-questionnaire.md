# Starting a new job

Answer the questions below and send them back. Everything after that is done
for you — the project row, the room plan, the rooms, and all the selections.

You do not need to touch Airtable to set a job up. If you would rather do it
yourself, the steps are at the bottom.

---

## Part 1 — The job

Ten answers. Most come straight off the contract.

1. **Job name** — how it should read to the owner, e.g. *Hahitti Residence*.
2. **Project code** — your internal number, e.g. *2510*.
3. **Address** — street, city, state, zip.
4. **Owner 1 name**
5. **Owner 2 name** — leave blank if there is only one.
6. **Project manager** — whoever at CKA runs this job.
7. **Construction start** — the date the job breaks ground.
   This is the one date everything else is counted from. Group 1 selections come
   due 30 days after it, group 2 at 60, group 3 at 90, group 4 at 120, group 5
   at 150. An estimate is fine; when the real date lands, say so and every date
   on the job moves with it.
8. **Roof type** — Tile, Flat, Metal, Shingle, or Mixed.
   A flat-roofed job is never asked to pick a tile color. Mixed keeps the tile
   question, because part of the house has tile.
9. **Who at CKA gets the daily email** — one address or several.
   This is the 6am digest of what the owner did yesterday. A job with nobody
   listed sends nothing.
10. **Owner email addresses** — **hold these back.**
    Do not send them until the job is built and you have walked the portal
    yourself. A blank owner email is the catch that stops anything reaching the
    client early. It is the last switch, not the first.

Names the owner recognises are worth more than tidy ones.

## Part 3 — Optional items

These exist in the library but arrive switched **off**, because most houses do
not have them. Tick the ones this job has. Anything you miss can be switched on
later in one click — nothing has to be rebuilt.

**Kitchen**
Range · Cooktop · Wall ovens · Steam oven · Warming drawer · Microwave or speed
oven · Coffee system · Ice maker · Beverage center · Wine storage · Undercounter
refrigeration · Freezer column · Second dishwasher · Prep sink · Prep faucet ·
Pot filler

*Range or cooktop is deliberately a choice — some kitchens have one, some the
other, some both.*

**Bath**
Tub · Freestanding tub filler · Steam generator and controls · Shower door pull

**Bedrooms**
Second closet system · Second closet lighting — for a primary with two closets,
or a bedroom with a his-and-hers

**Living / exercise room**
Ceiling fan

**Outdoor kitchen**
Vent hood

**Whole house**
Flooring – 2nd floor — only if the upstairs flooring differs from down

## Part 4 — What happens next

Once the answers come back:

1. The **project row** is created with everything from Part 1, minus the owner
   emails.
2. **Load starter plan** fills the Room Plan with one line per room type.
3. Your **counts** from Part 2 go in.
4. **Build rooms** creates every room and every selection, each with its due
   date already set from the construction start and its selection round.
5. The **optional items** from Part 3 are switched on.
5b. Any item with a **catalog** behind it (today that is the pool finish, which
   is set to Florida Stucco's Florida Gem line) has its colours loaded. The room
   build now ticks this itself - it used to be a manual step, and on the second
   job nobody remembered, so the owner opened the pool finish and read "CKA is
   putting options together" about a palette that had been ready for weeks.
6. You get the **portal link** and walk it yourself as if you were the owner.
7. Only then do the **owner emails** go in. That is what makes it live.

Steps 1-5 take a few minutes and need nothing from you.

## Doing it yourself

1. New row in **Projects**. Fill in the Part 1 fields. Leave owner emails blank.
2. Tick **Load starter plan** → the Room Plan fills with every room type at 0.
3. Type the counts on the Room Plan.
4. Tick **Build rooms** → rooms and selections are created. It only ever adds,
   so you can change a count and tick it again later for just the new rooms.
5. **Project setup → Optional items**: switch the ones this job has from
   *Not applicable* to *Not started*.
6. Copy the **Portal link** off the project row and open it.
7. Fill in the owner emails last.

If a count changes mid-job, change it and tick **Build rooms** again. If the
construction start changes, change it and tick **Recompute dates**. Neither
disturbs a decision the owner has already made.
